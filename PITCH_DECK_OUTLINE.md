# Pitch Deck — 6 slides (Canva / Google Slides)

**Slide 1 — Title**
Jan Awaaz: Citizen Voice to MP Action. Team name, Rishu Kumar & Ranjan Kumar Singh, Bihar National College. Track 1.

**Slide 2 — Problem**
- Complaints reach MPs via letters, calls, WhatsApp: scattered, unstructured
- No priority system, so urgent issues (broken bridge, no medicine, live wires) wait
- Citizens don't know if anyone heard them

**Slide 3 — Solution**
Citizen sends problem in Hindi/English → Gemini AI classifies, scores urgency, summarises, suggests action → MP sees a live dashboard.

**Slide 4 — Demo / Screenshots**
Screenshot of Citizen Portal + MP Dashboard (charts + Top priorities + AI briefing).

**Slide 5 — Tech & Impact**
Python, Streamlit, Gemini API. Impact: faster response, urgent issues first, less staff time, data-backed development planning.

**Slide 6 — Future scope + Team**
Voice + WhatsApp intake, more Indic languages, map view, pilot in one constituency. Team photos and contacts.
